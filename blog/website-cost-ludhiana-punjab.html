How much does a website cost in Ludhiana and Punjab?

Real numbers for 2026 — what a business website, an online store and a landing page cost, what moves the price, and what you keep paying after launch.

The short answer

For a business in Ludhiana or anywhere in Punjab, a properly built business website from a studio usually costs between ₹45,000 and ₹1,80,000, and takes three to five weeks once the content is ready. A simple site of five to fifteen pages sits at the lower end. An online store with a large catalogue, payments and stock sits at the upper end.

Those are the ranges we quote at Planorah, and they are a fair guide to what a site costs when it is built to load fast, show up on Google and be handed over to you completely. Below is what decides where your site falls in that range, and what it costs to keep running afterwards.

What changes the price

Two websites with the same number of pages can be priced very differently. These are the things that actually move the number:

  • Whether you sell online. A cart, checkout, payments through Razorpay or UPI, stock and order emails add the most work of anything on this list.
  • Who edits it after launch. If your team will update products, prices or posts themselves, the site needs a content management system with fields shaped to your content.
  • What it has to connect to. Tally, a CRM, WhatsApp order alerts, a booking calendar or Google Sheets each add a piece of integration work.
  • Design from scratch or adapted. A custom design for your brand takes longer than adapting an existing layout, but it is the difference between looking like your business and looking like a template.
  • Content. If the words and photos are ready, the site moves faster. Writing copy or arranging photography adds time.
  • Languages. A site in English and Hindi or Punjabi is more work than one language, mainly in content and testing.

Tip: the single most useful thing you can tell a studio is your budget. It changes what they recommend more than any other detail — and a good studio will tell you if something cheaper does the job.

What it costs to run

After launch, a website has small running costs that you pay to the providers directly. When the accounts are in your name, there is no margin added on top.

A maintenance retainer is optional. Many businesses run their site for months without one, and come back only when they want something new.

About very cheap websites

You will see websites offered for a few thousand rupees. Some are fine for what they are — a template with your name on it. Before you choose one, ask three questions:

  1. Whose name is the domain and hosting in? If it is the seller’s, you do not own your site, and moving it later can be difficult or impossible.
  2. How fast does it open on a phone over 4G? Most of your visitors in Punjab will arrive on a mid-range Android phone. A slow site loses them, and Google ranks it lower.
  3. Can it grow? If you will want online orders, a booking system or a second language next year, check that the site can take them without starting again.

Below about ₹40,000 a custom-built site rarely makes sense, because the cost of planning, building and handing it over properly is larger than the site itself. In that case a good template you run yourself is honestly the better choice.

How to get an accurate quote

You do not need a specification. A short message with these five things is enough to get a fixed price:

  • What your business does and who your customers are
  • What the website must do — show your work, take enquiries, sell online, take bookings
  • Whether your team will edit it after launch
  • Any website you like the look of
  • Your budget and the date you need it by

Common questions

Is GST included in website prices?

Prices are usually quoted before GST, and GST is added on the invoice where it applies. Ask any studio to confirm this in writing so there is no surprise on the final bill.

How long does it take to build a website?

Three to five weeks for most business websites, counted from the day the content is ready. A single landing page is usually under a week, and an online store with payments and a large catalogue takes six to eight weeks.

Will I own my website?

You should. Make sure the domain, hosting and source code are in your name. At Planorah every account is created in your name or transferred to it at handover, so you can move to another studio later without needing anything from us.

Do I need to pay every month for my website?

Only the small running costs — the domain once a year and hosting, which can be free for a small site. A maintenance retainer is optional.
