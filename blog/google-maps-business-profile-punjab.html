How to get your business on Google Maps in Punjab

When someone in Ludhiana searches for your trade, the first thing they see is a map with three businesses on it. Here is how to be one of them, step by step.

Why the map matters

Search for “sweet shop near me”, “dentist in Jalandhar” or “steel almirah Ludhiana” on a phone, and Google shows a map with a short list of businesses before any website. People tap the one that looks open, close and well reviewed, and call the number shown — often without ever visiting a website.

That list comes from Google Business Profile (it used to be called Google My Business). It is free, and for most shops, clinics, showrooms, institutes and restaurants in Punjab it brings in more customers than anything else online.

Eight steps to a listing that works

  1. Check whether you already have a listing. Search your business name and city on Google Maps. Customers or Google itself sometimes create one. If it exists, choose “Own this business?” to claim it rather than making a second one.
  2. Create the profile. Go to business.google.com with a Google account you will keep — ideally one in the business’s name, not a staff member’s personal account.
  3. Use your real business name. Exactly as it appears on your board and bills. Adding words like “Best Website Company Ludhiana” to the name breaks Google’s rules and can get the listing suspended.
  4. Choose the right primary category. This matters more than almost anything else. Pick the most specific one that fits — “Software company”, “Dental clinic”, “Sweet shop” — and add secondary categories only if they are genuinely true.
  5. Put the pin on your actual entrance. Google often drops the pin in the middle of a building or on the wrong street. Drag it to the door customers walk through. If you visit customers instead (a plumber, a tutor), set a service area and hide the address.
  6. Fill in every field. Phone number, website, opening hours (including holiday hours for Diwali and Gurpurab), services or products with prices, and a short description in plain language.
  7. Add real photos. The board outside, the inside of the shop, your team, your products. Listings with real photos get far more calls and direction requests than empty ones.
  8. Connect your website. Use the same name, address and phone number on your website as on the profile, so Google can match them up.

Tip: keep the name, address and phone number identical everywhere — the profile, your website, Justdial, IndiaMART, Facebook. Small differences confuse Google about which listing is the real you.

Verification, and how long it takes

Google needs to confirm that your business really exists at that address before your profile shows fully. Depending on your business, it offers one or more of these:

  • Video recording — usually the fastest. A short, continuous video showing your signboard, the street outside, the inside of your premises and something that proves you run it, like a counter, equipment or keys to a shutter.
  • Phone or email code — offered to some businesses, and instant when it is.
  • Postcard — the slowest. A card with a code is posted to your address and can take a couple of weeks, occasionally needing a second request.

In our experience verification takes between two days and two weeks, and nobody can speed it up. Start it on day one and get everything else ready while it runs.

What decides who shows first

Google says local results depend mainly on three things:

  • Relevance — how well your profile matches what was searched. This is where the right category, a complete description and a list of your services help.
  • Distance — how far you are from the person searching. You cannot change this, which is why an accurate pin matters.
  • Prominence — how well known and trusted you are. Reviews, a good website, and mentions of your business elsewhere online all count.

The practical part you control is reviews. Ask every happy customer — a WhatsApp message with your review link the same day works well — and reply to every review, good or bad, within a couple of days. Never buy reviews or offer discounts in exchange for them; it breaks Google’s rules and can get the profile removed.

Mistakes that hold listings back

  • Two or more listings for the same business, splitting your reviews
  • The pin on the wrong building, so customers ring you for directions
  • Opening hours that are wrong on holidays, so people arrive to a closed shutter
  • A personal Gmail of a staff member who has since left owning the profile
  • Keywords or city names stuffed into the business name
  • No photos, or only a logo

Common questions

Is a Google Business Profile free?

Yes. Creating, verifying and running a profile costs nothing. You only pay if you choose to run Google Ads or hire someone to set it up and manage it.

Can I list a business that works from home?

Yes. Set a service area — the cities or districts you cover — and hide your home address. Your profile can still show in local searches without your address being public.

How long until my business shows on Google Maps?

Usually within a few days of verification, which itself takes two days to two weeks. Ranking higher in the map results builds over weeks and months as reviews and photos accumulate.

Do I still need a website if I have a Google profile?

A profile alone gets you calls. A website alongside it — with the same name, address and phone — helps Google trust your listing, gives customers somewhere to see your full range, and lets you appear in searches the map does not cover.
