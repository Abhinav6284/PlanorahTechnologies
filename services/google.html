What we set up

Most local searches end on a Google listing, not on a website. Someone types your trade and their area, taps the first result that looks open and nearby, and calls the number shown. If that listing has the wrong pin, last year's hours or a category nobody searches for, the search still happens — it just ends somewhere else.

So we start with the listing and work outwards. The Business Profile that customers see on Search and Maps gets created or claimed, verified, and filled in completely. Then the measurement side — Search Console and Google Analytics 4 — goes on your website so you can see which searches brought people and what they did next. Then Google Workspace, so replies come from you@yourcompany.com instead of a free address that makes a real company look temporary.

We are also clear about the line between what we control and what Google controls. We control accuracy and completeness: name, categories, hours, service areas, photos, products, posts and how quickly reviews get answered. Google controls who appears where, and it changes that on its own schedule. Nobody can buy or promise a position. A verified, complete, consistently updated profile is the part you can actually influence — and it is the part most businesses never finish.

What’s included

  • Business Profile created or claimed, then taken through whichever verification Google asks for
  • Map pin moved to your actual entrance, with service areas set if you travel to customers
  • Primary and secondary categories chosen from what people search for, not what sounds impressive
  • Hours, holiday hours, phone, website and booking links, attributes, photos, logo and a products list
  • Duplicate and unclaimed listings found and merged, so two versions of you stop competing
  • Search Console and Analytics 4 connected, with events for calls, directions and form submissions
  • Workspace mailboxes on your own domain plus LocalBusiness schema on your site, so the name, address and phone match everywhere

Three parts

The listing, the numbers, the email.

These are usually bought from three different people and never quite line up. Set up together, they agree with each other and with your website.

Business Profile & Maps

Claim, verify, and fill in every field Google offers — categories, hours, service areas, attributes, photos and products. We check the pin against a real address on the map, merge duplicate listings, and set up a routine for posts and for replying to every review, good or bad, within a couple of days.

Search Console & Analytics

Search Console verified on your domain with the sitemap submitted, so you can see the queries you appear for and any page Google cannot index. Analytics 4 goes on next, with conversion events for calls, direction requests and form submissions — the actions worth counting, not raw visits.

Workspace email & domain

Mailboxes on your own domain, groups like accounts@ and support@, shared Drive folders, and old mail imported so nothing is left behind. DNS is the part people get wrong: we set MX, SPF, DKIM and DMARC properly, then test delivery in both directions before you switch.

Verification starts on day one.
