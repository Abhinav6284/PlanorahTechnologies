What we build

Most small companies do not have a data problem. They have a retyping problem. An order arrives on the website, someone copies it into a spreadsheet, someone else messages the customer on WhatsApp from a personal phone, and accounts hears about it on Friday. Every copy is a chance for the three systems to disagree, and by month-end they always do.

We build the connections that remove the retyping. A payment gateway that confirms the order the moment money is actually captured. A WhatsApp Business API sender that tells the customer what happened, from your verified business number rather than a staff member’s. An accounting sync that posts invoices and payments into Tally or Zoho Books on the schedule you choose. Where a system has a proper API we use it; where it does not, we use its import format and a scheduled job — and we tell you which of the two you are getting before you pay for it.

The part that decides whether any of this survives contact with a real month is what happens when a connection fails, because it will. Gateways have outages, tokens expire, a GST number gets rejected, an ERP is rebooted mid-sync. Every job we build retries with a back-off, records what it sent and what came back, and raises an alert to email or WhatsApp when it finally gives up. Failures become visible instead of silent — the difference between a two-minute fix and a reconciliation nightmare in the last week of the quarter.

How it runs

Test keys first, live keys last.

Nothing touches real money or real customers until the same flow has run end to end in a sandbox. Four to five weeks for a typical set of three connections.

Step 01

Map what exists

We list every system holding the same record today, who types into each one, and which is the source of truth when two disagree. That argument is settled on paper, not in code.

Days 1–3
Step 02

Build in a sandbox

Test gateway keys, a sample WhatsApp template, a copy of your Tally company. Each connection is built and replayed against fake data until the edge cases stop surprising us.

Weeks 1–3
Step 03

Go live, watched

We switch to production keys on a quiet day, put real transactions through end to end, and sit on the logs for the first few days of genuine volume rather than walking away.

Week 4
Step 04

Alerts & handover

Failure alerts point at your inbox, not only ours. You get the keys, the logs, the map of what talks to what, and 30 days of fixes on us.
