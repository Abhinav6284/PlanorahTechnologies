What we build

Most businesses need the same app on iPhone and Android. We build it once in React Native, share the screens and the logic across both platforms, and drop to native Swift or Kotlin only where the platform genuinely differs — camera pipelines, Bluetooth hardware, background location, biometric login. Two store listings come out of one codebase, so a change ships to both platforms in the same week rather than two months apart.

Phones lose signal, and we assume that from the first screen. Data is cached on the device, actions taken offline are queued, and everything syncs when the connection returns instead of failing behind a spinner. Push notifications are wired to deep links, so a tap opens the specific order or message rather than the home screen. Payments run through Razorpay, UPI or cards — or through Apple and Google in-app purchase where the stores require it for digital goods.

The release is part of the job, not something handed back to you at the end. We create the signing certificates and the Android keystore, write the listing copy, produce screenshots at every size both stores demand, complete the privacy and data-safety declarations, and submit for review. You supply two things: an Apple Developer Program account and a Google Play Console account, both registered to your business. Apple and Google verify organisation accounts against a D-U-N-S number and company documents, which takes several days, so we ask for them in week one rather than the week you want to launch.

What is included

  • Design for both platforms — iOS and Android patterns respected, not one layout skinned twice
  • A single codebase building to the App Store and Google Play from the same source
  • Native modules where the hardware demands them — camera, Bluetooth, biometrics, background location
  • Offline handling: local cache, queued actions and sync when the connection returns
  • Push notifications with deep links, plus crash reporting and analytics from day one
  • Payments through Razorpay, UPI and cards, or in-app purchase where the stores require it

Before you commit.

Ask anything else on WhatsApp or read the full FAQ.

Do we need our own Apple and Google developer accounts?

Yes, and they should be in your company’s name so the app is legally yours. Apple charges 99 US dollars a year for the Developer Program. Google charges a one-time 25 US dollars for a Play Console account. Both verify organisation accounts against a D-U-N-S number and business documents, which can take several days and occasionally longer if the registered address does not match. We walk you through the signup and then work inside your accounts — we never publish your app under ours, because that makes moving away from us expensive later.

How long does store review actually take?

Apple usually returns a decision within a day or two. Google Play is often similar for an established account, but a brand-new developer account goes through extra identity checks and the first release can sit for a week or more. Rejections are normal and almost always about metadata, permission justifications, login credentials for the reviewer or account deletion. We respond and resubmit at no extra cost until the app is approved.

React Native or fully native — which do we get?

React Native by default, because one team and one codebase costs you less and reaches both stores in the same week. Where an app is built around sustained camera work, heavy 3D, low-level Bluetooth or continuous background processing, we say so during scoping and quote native Swift and Kotlin instead. We would rather lose the shortcut than ship something that stutters on a mid-range Android phone.

What does it cost to keep the app running after launch?

Thirty days of fixes are included. After that most clients move to a monthly retainer of ₹15,000 – ₹45,000, which covers operating system updates, the yearly target-API deadlines both stores enforce, dependency and security upgrades, crash monitoring and small changes. You can also take the code and run it with your own team — the repository, the keys and the accounts are yours either way.
