Built in Ludhiana, for the whole state

Punjab runs on businesses that grew by word of mouth — a wholesaler in Ludhiana, a clinic in Jalandhar, a coaching institute in Patiala. Most of them now need the same things: to be found on Google, to take orders on a phone, and to stop running the day out of three spreadsheets and a diary.

That is the work we do. A website or online store that opens in a second on a mid-range phone and shows up when someone searches for your trade in your city. An Android and iOS app your customers can pay through with UPI. A CRM or billing system built around how your counter and your godown already work, with your Excel and Tally data moved across. And a Google Business Profile with the pin on your actual door.

The studio is at Chaudhary Complex on Rahon Road in Ludhiana. For clients in and around Ludhiana, Jalandhar and Amritsar we come in person for the parts that need it — the first meeting, staff training, kiosk installation, the week after launch. Everywhere else in Punjab, projects run over WhatsApp and video calls, with a working build on a link you can open every week.

Questions from Punjab businesses

Ask anything else on WhatsApp or read the full FAQ.

Do you work with businesses outside Ludhiana?

Yes. We work with businesses across Punjab — Jalandhar, Amritsar, Patiala, Mohali, Chandigarh, Bathinda and smaller towns — and with clients elsewhere in India. Around Ludhiana, Jalandhar and Amritsar we come in person for the parts that need it; everywhere else the project runs over WhatsApp and video calls, with a working build you can open every week.

How much does a website cost in Punjab?

Our websites start at ₹45,000 and most land between ₹45,000 and ₹1,80,000, depending on the number of pages, whether you sell online, and what it has to connect to. You get a fixed price in writing within 48 hours of sending your requirement, and that is the price you pay.

Can you make an app for my shop or business?

Yes — Android and iOS from one codebase, with UPI and card payments, push notifications and an admin panel for your staff. Mobile apps typically take 6 to 10 weeks. We handle the Play Store and App Store submissions and put both accounts in your name.

Do you speak Punjabi and Hindi?

Yes. Calls, WhatsApp messages and training sessions can be in Punjabi, Hindi or English — whichever your team is most comfortable with.

Will my business show up on Google Maps?

We create or claim your Google Business Profile, get it verified, put the pin on your real entrance, fill in hours, photos and categories, and connect it to your website — the things that decide whether you appear when someone nearby searches for your trade.

Tell us what your business needs.

A few lines on WhatsApp is enough — what it should do, who will use it, and by when. You get a fixed quote within 48 hours, wherever you are in Punjab.
